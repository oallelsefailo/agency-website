What is manual reporting actually costing you?

Your tools are free, and you will still open GA4, Search Console, and the Magento admin for the deep dives. What costs money is the repetitive part: pulling the same numbers out of all three every week and reconciling them into one view for your review. That recurring assembly is the job Vysible removes, not the analysis itself. Put in your own numbers below.

This is an estimate, not a quote. The hours and the hourly rate are yours; everything else is an assumption we have written down so you can argue with it. We treat a month as 4.3 weeks, assume a dashboard removes about 70% of the recurring time spent combining sources (you still have to read the thing), and include a typical $400 one-time setup fee in the year-one figure. It counts only that recurring assembly time, not the deeper analysis you would still do. Your own numbers will differ, and if the result looks too good it probably is.

That time goes away when the sources are already connected.
